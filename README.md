# Pogee AI - The Psychology of AI Companionship

Research notes on the psychology of AI companionship and emotional design. See https://pogee.ai

## Highlights

### Consistency

Predictable personalities build trust faster than raw intelligence.

### Reciprocity

Being remembered creates the feeling of a two-way relationship.

### Boundaries

Healthy limits make a companion more trustworthy, not less fun.
